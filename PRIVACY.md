# Iblock privacy policy

Iblock does not collect, sell or share any data.

- Your settings, scroll counts, time watched, locks, badges and password hash are stored only in your browser's local extension storage, on your device.
- Iblock makes no network requests. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- Iblock reads the address and page of the supported sites (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Facebook, and X, Reddit and Pinterest if you turn them on) only to detect short-form feeds, count them and block them. It doesn't run on any other site.
- Uninstalling Iblock or using "Reset statistics" deletes this data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Eraxty/Iblock/issues
