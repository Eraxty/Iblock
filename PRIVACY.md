# Iblock privacy policy

Iblock does not collect, sell or share any data.

- Your settings, scroll counts, time watched, locks, badges and password hash are stored only in your browser's local extension storage, on your device. So are short coded IDs of the shorts you watched today, so scrolling back to one doesn't count it twice; they're cleared every day.
- Iblock makes no network requests. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- Iblock reads the address and page of the supported sites (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Facebook, X, Reddit and Pinterest) only to detect short-form videos and feeds, count them and block them. Sites you switch off in settings are left alone. It doesn't run on any other site.
- Uninstalling Iblock or using "Reset statistics" deletes this data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Eraxty/Iblock/issues
